How it works

Four steps from your drop to a published channel format episode.

  1. Drop it in

    Hand GLOWHUM a topic or a research report. A topic triggers grounded research with a citation check before anything is written.

  2. Script and shot table

    The report becomes an episode skeleton with beats and timings, then a shot table where every shot carries its lane and its source.

  3. Produce on our lanes

    Voice, motion, screen records, cut, captions and music run on our own GPU lanes. Claims keep their citations on screen.

  4. Publish and receipt

    The episode goes to YouTube, three verticals go to TikTok and Shorts, and you get a receipt with the unit cost printed on it.
